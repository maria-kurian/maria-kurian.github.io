01

Chapter 01 — Money Aug 2024 — present

Learning to speak money.

After a degree spent reading the world, I wanted to understand the economy that runs it, and to get financially smarter myself. So the literature graduate walked into a bank.

I'm a Relationship Manager on the Wealth & Trade desk at ICICI Bank's MG Road branch in Kochi: Kerala's first ICICI branch, and India's 10th. It's a high-stakes branch and the work is wealth management sales. I look after almost 200 high-net-worth clients, individuals and corporate entities alike, and find where fixed deposits, loans and investments can add real value for each of them.

Chapter 02 — Words Mar 2023 — Mar 2024

Editing at the speed of esports.

Esports was my crash course in reading an industry from the inside: its money, its careers, its growing pains. I learned it at the desk of analyst and commentator Ranjit “MambaSR” Patel.

As his Editorial Assistant I edited and proofread pieces on market growth, career trajectories and stakeholder insights, ran the editorial calendars and drove the newsletter's KPIs. It was also my introduction to market research, industry analysis and financial journalism in Indian esports.

Chapter 03 — Sound Jul 2021 — Dec 2022

Behind the mixing desk.

My first deep dive into entertainment. In the studio I learned that creativity, technical production and marketing aren't three jobs. They're one song.

As Production Associate to producer, singer and songwriter Joshua “Jay Former” Dixon, I worked on audio engineering, technical editing and mixing, hands-on in digital audio workstations, plus the music videos that went with the releases. I also ran his social media strategy and marketing.
