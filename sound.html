I

Movement one — Training

I trained my ears before I trained my eye.

Nearly thirteen years of professional training in singing and keyboard, across jazz and classical.

Jazz is the art of improvising around a structure. Classical is the art of honouring one. Both are mostly about timing, which, it turns out, is also true of a headline, a caption and a campaign. My early grades were taken as a student of Dolce School of Music.

II

Movement two — Studio · Jul 2021 – Dec 2022

Then I went behind the glass.

Eighteen months in the studio with producer, singer and songwriter Jay Former. I sat at the desk for audio engineering, technical editing and mixing, and learned to sample music on a DAW until the grid felt like a second stave.

It's where I understood the synergy between creativity, technical production and marketing. Not three jobs, really. One song, played in three parts.
