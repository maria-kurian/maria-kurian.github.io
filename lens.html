03Room 03 — The Lens

Things I found beautiful.

Photography came to me late, and it gives me away more than anything else I make. These are the moments I kept: shot on a phone, edited by hand, hung here so I can look again.

Wall text

Everything I make begins with raw desire. Some days it is the urge to say something. Other days I just want to look at something lovely for a while.

Photography arrived later than everything else, and oddly it is the one that leaves me most exposed. A camera has a way of revealing the person behind it, and how simple or complicated their wants really are.

So my pictures don't reach for the abstract. They tell simple stories. They are beautiful things, or rather, things I found beautiful, kept so I can come back and indulge in them at leisure.
